\# Global Olympics Sports Performance Analysis (1992–2016)

An interactive Tableau dashboard analyzing global fuel price fluctuations (Petrol, Diesel, LPG, and Brent Crude Oil) and regional taxation benchmarks across 80+ nations.

\## 📌 Project Overview

\* \*\*Data Granularity:\*\* Weekly fuel pricing data across countries from 2020 to 2026.

\* \*\*Core KPI Metrics:\*\*

\* Medal Totals Across Olympic Hosts

\* Average Medal Efficiency % by Region

\* Avg Medals per Athlete by Sport

\* Regional Medal Efficiency & Distribution by Sport

\## 🔍 Key Dashboard Features

1\. \*\*Avg Medals per Athlete by Sport\*\*

2\. \*\*Trend of Total Medals Over the Years (1992–2016)\*\*

3\. \*\*Performance Metrics by Country\*\*

4\. \*\*Sport Investment vs Medal Performance\*\*

5\. \*\*Regional Medal Efficiency & Distribution by Sport\*\*
